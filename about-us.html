About Us

Welcome to L'Atelier Patisserie, where tradition meets
creativity in the heart of Vancouver.

Our Beginning

Founded in 2015, L'Atelier Patisserie was born out of a passion for
crafting exquisite pastries that delight the senses. Our journey
began with a small team of dedicated bakers, each bringing
their unique expertise and love for the art of patisserie.
From humble beginnings, we have grown into a beloved
destination for those seeking the finest baked goods in
Vancouver.
